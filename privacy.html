PAPERWISE POLICIES

Your privacy.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

We collect the account email and identifiers, order history, UPI transaction reference submitted for manual verification, and basic technical logs needed to provide the service, protect accounts, and investigate support requests. Paperwise does not ask customers to enter card details, UPI PINs, passwords, or payment one-time codes into this website.

How we use it

We use account and order information to manage purchases, review submitted payment references, provide account access, prevent fraud, answer support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Payments, downloads and security

UPI payments are made through the customer's chosen UPI app to the destination displayed at checkout. Submitted transaction references remain pending until an administrator checks the actual payment independently. PDFs are kept in private storage; the server issues an expiring download link only after an order is marked paid.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable through the published support contact. Before launch, this policy must be completed with the operating entity, contact address, processing jurisdiction, retention practices and other legally required details.
