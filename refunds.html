PAPERWISE POLICIES

Refunds, simply.

Last updated: October 2026

Digital purchases

Because each PDF is a digital product, purchases are reviewed individually. If a payment was duplicated, a download is unavailable, or an edition is materially defective, contact support with the order details. A submitted UPI reference alone is not payment confirmation and does not unlock a download.

Review and timing

For a refund request, Paperwise will check the order and payment record. If a refund is approved for a manual UPI purchase, the administrator will contact the customer to coordinate the refund and confirm the expected timing. Refunds are not automatic or instant.

Before launch

This policy must be confirmed by the store owner against the actual refund process and applicable consumer-protection requirements. Publish a working support contact before accepting customer orders.
